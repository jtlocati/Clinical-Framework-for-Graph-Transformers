Task 8 conclusion summary

Task 8 evaluated training-time fairness methods using train-split race-weighted loss and subgroup-loss variance regularization.

Final model choices:
- LSTM: keep train_race_weighted with lambda = 0.0
- TGTFull: keep train_race_weighted_varreg005 with lambda = 0.05

Main findings:
- For LSTM, subgroup variance regularization did not provide a clear enough cross-seed fairness-performance improvement over train_race_weighted alone. Mean AUROC and AUPRC changed only slightly, and disparity changes were mixed across attributes.
- For TGTFull, subgroup variance regularization at lambda = 0.05 improved mean overall AUROC and AUPRC relative to train_race_weighted alone, while still remaining below the original unadjusted TGTFull model on overall performance.
- This means Task 8 supports different final training-time settings by model family: weighted-only for the baseline model, and weighted-plus-variance-regularization for the graph-transformer model.

Task 8 final recommended settings:
- Baseline model: LSTM_train_race_weighted
- Graph-transformer model: TGTFull_train_race_weighted_varreg005
