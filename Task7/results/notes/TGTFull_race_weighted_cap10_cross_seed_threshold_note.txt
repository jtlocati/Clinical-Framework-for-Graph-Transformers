Post-hoc threshold tuning for TGTFull_race_weighted_cap10 was evaluated on seeds 42, 123, and 2024 using candidate global thresholds 0.40, 0.45, and 0.50. Across seeds, 0.45 provided the best overall fairness-performance compromise relative to the default threshold of 0.50. It consistently improved overall F1 and reduced several subgroup disparity measures, especially for age and race-related gap metrics, while only modestly reducing overall accuracy.
